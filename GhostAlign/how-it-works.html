How GhostAlign Eliminates Angle Drift

A simple 3-step workflow engineered for the job site. Works 100% offline with zero cloud lag.

STEP 1: INITIAL WALKTHROUGH

Snap the "Before" Photo

When arriving at the customer's property or vehicle, open GhostAlign and snap your initial walkthrough photos. Enter client contact details (name, phone, vehicle/property notes) to keep every job organized.

⚡ 100% On-Device · Works in basements & rural areas with no signal

STEP 2: PRECISION ALIGNMENT

Line Up the Live Ghost Overlay

When the job is done, open the angle in GhostAlign. The app projects your original before photo as a semi-transparent "ghost" directly over your live camera viewfinder. Slide the opacity bar, line up the body lines, and tap shutter for a millimeter-perfect match.

🎯 Rule-of-thirds grid & crosshairs ensure zero perspective jump

STEP 3: DISPUTE PROTECTION

Export the Branded Client PDF

Review your matched pair in the 50/50 comparison studio, then tap "Export Client PDF". GhostAlign instantly formats a clean, timestamped inspection report with your company logo and license number to text or email directly to the homeowner.

📄 Text, email, or AirDrop to settle disputes in 30 seconds
