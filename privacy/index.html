Privacy Policy

100% Local-First & Zero-Data Collection Guarantee

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by rad2484, including but not limited to:

  • RadQuotes (Fast On-Site Estimates & Change Orders)
  • RadShot (Before & After Ghost Overlay Camera)
  • RadTradeCalc (Jobsite Material & Waste Calculator)
  • DrivewayReview (5-Star Google Review SMS Booster)
  • RadDeposit (Client Deposit & Booking Confirmation)
  • TaxBinder (Receipt Matcher & Mileage Log)
  • ProvenanceVault (Collector Proof of Ownership Book)
  • SanitizePDF (Offline Document Blackout & ID Watermarker)
  • CancelVault (Offline Subscription Tracker & Cancel Directory)

1. Zero-Data Collection Principle

Our applications are built on a strict Local-First architecture. We do not operate central database servers, and we do not collect, harvest, monitor, sell, or rent your personal, business, or financial data.

  • Estimates, Quotes & Bookings: Customer names, addresses, line items, and signatures are stored strictly on your local device.
  • Photos & Camera: Before/After photos and document scans are processed on your device's GPU/CPU and saved in your device sandbox. We never transmit your images to external servers.
  • Financial Data & Receipts: Bank CSV files, receipts, and mileage logs are parsed client-side and remain 100% private to your phone.

2. Device Permissions Used

Depending on the specific app, the following standard permissions may be requested solely to provide device functionality:

  • Camera: Used in RadShot, RadDeposit, and TaxBinder exclusively to capture photos, scans, and signatures on-device.
  • Storage / Filesystem: Used to export PDFs, backup files, and save generated collages to your device.
  • Local Notifications: Used in CancelVault and RadDeposit to set on-device reminders before free trials convert or appointments occur.

3. Third-Party Services & Cloud Backups

Our apps do not track you across third-party apps or websites. When you choose to backup your device or export files, you do so using your own personal accounts (such as Apple iCloud, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or native email), subject to your respective provider's privacy terms.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our developer GitHub profile at github.com/rad2484.
