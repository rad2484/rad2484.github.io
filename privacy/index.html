SUMMARY IN PLAIN ENGLISH: We do not track you. We do not operate remote databases storing your records. We do not embed ad-tracking networks, behavioral profiling tools, or third-party analytics SDKs. All of your quotes, photos, receipts, mileage logs, serial numbers, and redacted files remain 100% on your device hardware.

1. Zero Remote Data Collection Principle

Our applications are built on an uncompromising Local-First, Air-Gapped Architecture. When you use our applications:

  • No Central Servers: We operate NO central servers, cloud APIs, or hosted databases that receive, store, or process your client details, prices, or documents.
  • No Account Creation: You do not need to create an account, register an email address, or provide phone numbers to use the core features of our applications.
  • No Telemetry or Profiling: We do not track your screen views, clicks, keystrokes, or physical location.

2. Device Hardware Permissions & Usage

The Applications only request native operating system permissions strictly necessary to deliver local user-initiated functionality:

  • Camera: Used in GhostAlign, RadDeposit, and TaxBinder solely to capture real-time photos, overlay alignment frames, scan receipts, or record client signatures. Camera streams and images are processed strictly on your device's local processor and never sent to external servers.
  • Photo Library & Local Storage: Used to allow you to select existing photos for before/after comparisons, save generated PDF quotes/invoices to your device storage, and export encrypted backup archives (.radvault).
  • Local Notifications: Used in CancelVault and RadDeposit to set on-device scheduled reminders (such as alerting you before a free trial renewal date or prior to an upcoming appointment). These notifications are handled 100% locally by iOS/Android and do not communicate with remote push servers.
  • SMS Deep-Linking: Used in DrivewayReview to launch your device's native messaging application with a pre-composed template. The Developer never sees, routes, or logs your SMS messages or recipient contacts.

4. Third-Party Platform Services & Cloud Backups

  • In-App Purchases & Payments: All commercial transactions and lifetime unlocks are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. (Apple App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play Store). The Developer never has access to your credit card numbers, payment instruments, or billing addresses.

5. Security of Your Data

Because all data remains on your physical hardware, your data security depends directly on your device security practices. We strongly recommend enabling strong device passcodes, biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint), and full-disk device encryption.
