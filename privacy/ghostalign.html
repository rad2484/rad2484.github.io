100% Local-First & Zero Cloud Collection

GhostAlign Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

GhostAlign ("the App") is developed and maintained by Rahul Damodaran. GhostAlign is built on a local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your business records, job-site photos, and customer details are strictly your property.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data or photos.

  • Photos and Job Records: All before-and-after photos, alignment overlays, project notes, customer names, and contact details are stored strictly locally on your device within sandboxed storage (IndexedDB). They are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use GhostAlign.
  • Offline Functionality: The App works 100% offline without an active internet connection.

3. Device Permissions

GhostAlign requests only the minimum device permissions necessary to operate:

  • Camera: Required to capture before and after photos and display the live ghost alignment viewfinder. Camera frames are processed strictly in real-time on your device's GPU and are never recorded without your explicit shutter tap.
  • Photo Library (Optional): Required only if you choose to import an existing photo from your device's camera roll or save generated PDF reports/collages to your camera roll.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

All in-app purchases (such as GhostAlign Lifetime Pro) are processed securely through Apple App Store (In-App Purchase) or Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card, billing address, or payment details. Apple and Google handle all transaction processing under their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Analytics and Tracking

GhostAlign contains zero third-party tracking scripts, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry. We do not track your location, device identifier (IDFA/GAID), or usage patterns.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
